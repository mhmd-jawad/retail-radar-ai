IE1 — Market Intelligence
=========================

IE1 processes competitor price signals in real time. It normalises scraped
records from 7 Apify actors (covering 8 Lebanese retailers) and exposes the
``CompetitorSignals`` shape consumed by IE2.

Runs on **port 8001** (owner: Mohammad Jawad).
